How Bubble Sort Works

Bubble sort repeatedly steps through the array, comparing each pair of neighboring bars. If the left bar is taller than the right one, they swap places — just like a bubble drifting to the surface.

Every full pass pushes the largest remaining value to its correct spot at the end of the array, which is why it turns green. The algorithm keeps making passes until an entire pass happens with no swaps, meaning the array is fully sorted.
